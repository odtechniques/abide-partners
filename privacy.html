Abide

Privacy notice

Abide is a personal accountability app in a private pilot. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The Abide pilot is run by John (odtechniques), who decides how the data below is used. To ask anything about your data, reply to any email from Abide.

If you use Abide on your phone

Almost everything stays on your phone: your rules and history, journal, prayers, goals, affirmations, memory verses, sermon notes, takeaways and recordings. It's encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware and is never sent to the server.

If you connect accountability partners, the server stores:

  • your name and email address
  • your partners' names, email addresses and what you've chosen to share with each
  • 48-hour uninstall requests and your partners' replies and notes
  • check-in requests, milestones you share, and encouragement your partners send
  • the weekly summary totals you share (for example minutes on managed apps, Bible reading minutes, steps followed through)
  • when your phone last checked in, and whether its protections (home screen, usage access, app-switch watcher, website lock) are on

Backups you create are encrypted with your own passphrase and saved wherever you choose. Nobody else can open them.

If you're someone's accountability partner

We store your name and email address (given by the person who invited you), whether you accepted, and any replies, notes or encouragement you send through the partner page. You only receive what that person has chosen to share. You never see their journal, prayers or recordings, and you can't change anything on their phone.

Why, and the legal basis

  • To run the accountability features the person using Abide asked for: sending invitations, requests, check-ins, summaries and alerts. Basis: legitimate interests (helping someone keep the commitments they chose), and for partners, your choice to accept the invitation.
  • To keep the service secure and working. Basis: legitimate interests.

Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone else.

Who helps provide the service

  • Supabase hosts the server and database (region: London).
  • Brevo sends the emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this page and the partner page.

How long it's kept

  • Unanswered invitations expire after 14 days.
  • Everything on the server is kept while the account is active, and deleted when the person using Abide deletes their partner account (in the app: Menu → Your data & privacy). Partners are told by email.
  • Data on the phone is deleted when Abide is uninstalled, unless a backup was made.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by replying to any Abide email. Partners can also stop receiving emails at any time by asking the person who invited them, or by replying to say so. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
